Which file?

PowerPoint, Keynote: SVG (best) or PNG.
Word, Pages, OmniGraffle: PDF or PNG.
Web, Canva, Figma, Illustrator: SVG.
Email signatures, quick drops: JPG (background baked in).
Not sure: ALL (zip) has every format.
